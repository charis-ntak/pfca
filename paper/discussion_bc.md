On the breast cancer dataset ([[tab:phaseb]]) the picture that emerges is one of a trade off that the method makes explicit rather than one of uniform superiority. PFCA removed the model output faster than any feature level baseline when its concepts were deleted in order of importance, with a deletion area of 0.639 against 0.814 for SHAP, and its ranking of concept importance was perfectly stable across resamples, whereas feature level SHAP and bootstrapped SHAP reached a rank stability of 0.507 on a dataset in which every measured characteristic appears as a mean, a standard error and a worst value. However, the insertion area and the surrogate fidelity of PFCA were below those of feature level SHAP. These two findings have the same cause: the knee point of the front selected two concepts and retained 1.4 of them, so that the surrogate of PFCA was fitted on at most two coarse quantities whereas the matched feature level surrogate used 20.8 features whose exact Shapley values sum to the output by construction. In line with the systematic review of {@nauta2023anecdotal}, faithfulness and compactness pull in opposite directions, and the front of Section 3.4 shows that an explanation with three concepts would have recovered most of the fidelity at the cost of complexity. The low perturbation stability of LIME in the same table reflects its negative surrogate fidelity rather than a desirable property, since a surrogate that does not track the output cannot be unstable with respect to it, a pattern consistent with the instability of local linear explanations documented by {@visani2022statistical}.

The questionnaire results ([[tab:phasec]]) illustrate what the concept level adds in a setting with known constructs. The a priori ablation and the grouped baseline, which both use the five subscales as crisp groups, reached a surrogate fidelity of 0.694, whereas the data driven PFCA partition reached 0.890 with two concepts, which suggests that the correlation structure of the simulated items, in which the first two constructs interact and the constructs correlate at 0.3, is better summarized by a coarser partition than the questionnaire design implies. The adjusted Rand index of 0.347 between the hardened partition and the subscales is therefore a property of the knee point, which pays for every additional concept in complexity, rather than a failure to detect the constructs: the rumination subscale was recovered with an overlap of 0.865 ([[tab:reliability]]), and the second concept of the example explanation coincided with it exactly ([[fig:phasec_overlap]]). The reliability table also contains the result that motivated the questionnaire phase, namely that constructs differ in how reliably they are attributed. The self efficacy and social support concepts carried relative support widths of 2.07 and 1.95 and disagreement indices of 0.146 and 0.190, against 1.28 and 0.110 for anxiety, and the fraction of type 2 attributions followed the same order. Feature level SHAP reports none of these differences, and bootstrapped SHAP summed over the items of a subscale ordered the constructs differently, with the widest interval on social support (2.55) and the narrowest on anxiety (0.83), which indicates that the uncertainty of a sum of item level attributions and the uncertainty of a concept attribution are not the same quantity. This agrees with the argument of {@au2022grouped} that grouped importance has to be defined at the group level rather than assembled from feature level values, and with the observation of {@molnar2022general} that feature level interpretations under dependence conceal their own uncertainty.

The example front ([[fig:front]]) and the diagnostics of [[tab:front]] address the risk, stated in the study design, that the three objectives would not conflict and the front would collapse to one solution. The front held between 12.8 and 16.4 distinct explanations per run on the real data, fidelity loss and complexity were strongly negatively correlated over the feasible configurations (-0.78 to -0.84), and no run collapsed. The positive correlation between complexity and instability (+0.60) indicates that the two objectives partly agree, so that the front is shaped mainly by the fidelity against complexity trade off, and the knee point consistently settled on the simplest configuration beyond which fidelity loss stops falling steeply. The three explanations drawn from the front in [[fig:front]] show the practical consequence: the same instance is described by one coarse negative concept at the knee and by three signed concepts at the most faithful member, and the interval type 2 upgrade of the knee explanation records that the two model classes disagreed on the magnitude of the dominant concept, information that a single SHAP vector cannot carry [@fisher2019all; @slack2021reliable].

The sensitivity analysis ([[tab:sensitivity]]) separates the design choices that matter from those that do not. The number of resamples affected the width and the coverage of the fuzzy numbers rather than the point attribution: the relative deviation of the quantile arrays from those at $B = 50$ halved from $B = 10$ to $B = 20$ and the support coverage rose accordingly, which indicates that 20 resamples are the smallest adequate pool at this problem size and that the full study value of 100 is conservative, consistent with the stabilization of bootstrap intervals for explanations reported by {@slack2021reliable}. The number of model classes acted on a different quantity, the disagreement index, since a third class with a linear structure raised the number of type 2 attributions fivefold and lowered the rank stability, which is the behavior the type 2 upgrade was designed to expose rather than a degradation of the method [@fisher2019all]. The shape of the fuzzy number and the percentile levels moved the coverage in the direction expected from their definitions, the loading distance produced partitions closer to the factor structure on these cells at the price of stability, and the evolutionary solver recovered the exhaustive front and its knee in every run at a lower cost, which supports the use of NSGA-II when the grid becomes large. The one choice that changed the explanation itself was the knee criterion. The utopia criterion selected two concepts where the hyperplane criterion selected four to five, and the recovery error against a five factor truth fell from 1.154 to 0.642 with the latter, a trade off that the front makes visible but that the default knee resolves toward simplicity. Finally, the alternative label sets changed the wording of the explanation without changing its substance, since the sign of every label agreed with the default set in at least 83.6 percent of the attributions and the selection metrics were unchanged by construction, which answers the concern that the labels are arbitrary [@mencar2019paving].
